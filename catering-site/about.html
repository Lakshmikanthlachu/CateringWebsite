Our Story

Started in a home kitchen, grown by word of mouth

Bellavita began in 2008 when founder and executive chef Arjun Malhotra started cooking for friends' weddings out of his family's kitchen. Word spread quickly, and within five years the business had grown into a full catering studio serving weddings, corporate events and festive celebrations across the city.

Today we're a team of over 40 chefs, coordinators and service staff — but the philosophy hasn't changed: every menu is designed around the people eating it, not the other way around.
